# Social Control Theory

## Summary

Travis Hirschi’s social control theory (1969), as described by the source, associates crime and delinquency with weak ties to conventional norms and institutions. It asks what restrains offending rather than assuming conformity needs no explanation.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source characterises weak social bonds as an explanation; it does not provide evidence establishing causation or enumerate the components of the theory.
