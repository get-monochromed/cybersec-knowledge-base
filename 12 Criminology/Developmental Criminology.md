# Developmental Criminology

## Summary

The developmental approach studies changes and influences across the life course that may contribute to antisocial or criminal behaviour. It considers risk factors alongside protective factors that may buffer risk.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source gives examples including nutrition, loss of a parent, school failure and housing as risk factors, and a caring mentor, social skills and resilience as protective factors. These examples are not deterministic and their effects depend on evidence and context.
