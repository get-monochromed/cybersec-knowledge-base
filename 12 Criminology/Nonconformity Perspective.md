# Nonconformity Perspective

## Summary

A criminological starting assumption that, without social constraints, people are disposed to violate conventions. The source presents biological predispositions and social control as illustrations, not as proof of innate criminality.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source’s framing of human nature is a theoretical assumption, not an established universal fact. Claims about malnutrition and environmental exposure need independent verification and do not imply inevitability.
