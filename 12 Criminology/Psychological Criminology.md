# Psychological Criminology

## Summary

Psychological criminology studies the behaviour and mental processes of people who commit crime, including how behaviour is acquired, evoked, maintained and modified. The source considers social, personality, cognitive, biological and developmental influences.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The contrast with sociological criminology is a difference in emphasis, not a claim that either discipline ignores the other level of analysis.
