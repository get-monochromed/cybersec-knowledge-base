# Learning Perspective

## Summary

The learning perspective characterises humans as initially neither inherently conforming nor unruly and as developing throughout life; it emphasises the social environment in learning behaviour and beliefs.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source’s claim that virtually all behaviour is learned is a broad theoretical position, not a demonstrated exclusion of biological or other influences.
