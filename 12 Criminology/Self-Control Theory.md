# Self-Control Theory

## Summary

Gottfredson and Hirschi’s General Theory of Crime (1990), also called self-control theory, identifies deficient self-control or self-regulation as central to crime and delinquency.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source reports the contested claim that self-control is established by about age eight and remains stable, but also cites research suggesting later development. These are second-hand citations, not a settled conclusion.
