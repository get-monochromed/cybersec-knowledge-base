# Biological Approach (Criminology)

## Summary

Biological and neurological approaches examine possible contributions of biological, genetic and neuropsychological factors to behaviour, including aggression. The source notes that traumatic brain injury can produce personality changes including increased aggression.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The excerpt supplies no study details or effect sizes. Biological factors and traumatic brain injury do not determine offending; an association or possible change does not establish causation in an individual.
