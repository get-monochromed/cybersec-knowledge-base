# General Strain Theory

## Summary

In developing **General Strain Theory (GST)** in 1992, Agnew used the word *strain* in a slightly different way: strains are **events and conditions that are disliked by individuals**, not simply blocked access to culturally prized goals. The inability to achieve one's goals is only one such condition. Others include **losing something of value** and **being treated negatively by others** (2006).

That reframing widens [[Strain Theory]] well beyond the materialistic-goals version and, in effect, turns it into a general account of how adverse experience translates into offending.
