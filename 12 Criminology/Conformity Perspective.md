# Conformity Perspective

## Summary

The **conformity perspective** views humans as creatures of conformity who want to do the "right" thing. To a large extent this assumption is the foundation of the humanistic perspectives in psychology: human beings are basically "good" people trying to live to their fullest potential. The related branch called **positive psychology** studies the individual characteristics that make life worth living, such as contentment and intimacy.

Its criminological consequence is a question rather than an answer: if people are disposed to conform, the interesting problem is not why some offend but why conformity fails — which is the ground [[Strain Theory]] occupies, and the opposite of the determinist starting point in [[Positivist Theory]].
