# Cognitive Approach (Criminology)

## Summary

The cognitive approach examines attitudes, beliefs, values and thoughts in relation to criminal behaviour. The source describes examples in which offenders minimise harm or justify their actions.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source makes broad claims about distorted cognition among serious offenders and particular offence groups without supporting evidence in the excerpt. Such patterns cannot be assumed of every offender or treated as established causes.
