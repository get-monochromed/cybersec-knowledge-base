# Sociological Criminology

## Summary

Sociological criminology studies relationships between crime and group or social variables, situational conditions, and the distribution of power, including how laws define crime and are enforced.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The source reports relationships involving demographic variables but provides no effect sizes, methods or controls. Group associations must not be treated as causes or used to infer individual offending.
