# Differential Association Theory

## Summary

Differential association theory holds that criminal behaviour, like other social behaviour, is learned through interaction with other people. The source emphasises examples and messages received from others.

> [!warning] Unverified
> The excerpt ends mid-sentence; it does not provide a complete account. Its apparent exclusion of other influences is a theoretical claim, not established evidence that other factors never contribute.
