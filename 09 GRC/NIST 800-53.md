# NIST SP 800-53

## Summary

A very large catalogue of security and privacy controls, mandatory for US federal systems and widely borrowed elsewhere (including as the basis for many commercial control sets). If you need a control for a specific problem, there is probably an AC-, AU-, SC-, or SI- family entry for it.

## Control families (Rev. 5, 20 families)

AC access control · AT awareness and training · AU audit and accountability · CA assessment, authorization, monitoring · CM configuration management · CP contingency planning · IA identification and authentication · IR incident response · MA maintenance · MP media protection · PE physical and environmental · PL planning · PM program management · PS personnel security · PT PII processing and transparency · RA risk assessment · SA system and services acquisition · SC system and communications protection · SI system and information integrity · SR supply chain risk management.

## Structure

Each control has a baseline identifier, description, supplemental guidance, and **control enhancements** (e.g. AC-2(1) automated account management). Controls are selected into baselines (Low/Moderate/High impact) using **FIPS 199** categorisation.

## RMF (Risk Management Framework, 800-37)

1. **Prepare** · 2. **Categorize** the system · 3. **Select** controls · 4. **Implement** · 5. **Assess** · 6. **Authorize** (ATO) · 7. **Monitor** continuously.

This is the original "continuous authorization" model; [[NIST CSF]] is the voluntary, friendlier cousin.

## How to use it outside US federal

- As a control reference library when writing internal standards.
- As a mapping source: FedRAMP, CMMC, and many sector frameworks derive from it.
- Pair with the **OSCAL** machine-readable format if you need automation.
- Do not attempt full implementation unless required — 800-53 is exhaustive and expensive; start from [[CIS Controls]] IG1 for practical prioritisation.
