# Foundations

The vocabulary everything else is built on. If a later note feels confusing, it is usually a gap here.

## Core concepts

- [[CIA Triad]] — confidentiality, integrity, availability; the three properties security protects
- [[Authentication, Authorization and Accounting]] — proving identity, granting permission, recording action
- [[Kerberos]] — the ticket protocol behind Active Directory, and the root of every AD attack
- [[Security Principles]] — least privilege, fail-safe defaults, separation of duties
- [[Security Controls]] — preventive, detective, corrective, compensating
- [[Defence in Depth]] — layered controls so one failure is not fatal
- [[Attack Surface]] — everything an attacker can touch
- [[Trust Boundaries]] — where data crosses from lower to higher trust

## Analysis and decision-making

- [[Vulnerability, Threat, Risk and Exploit]] — the four words people misuse most
- [[Threat Modelling]] — STRIDE, attack trees, kill chains
- [[Risk Management]] — likelihood × impact, treatment options, residual risk
- [[Security Models]] — Bell-LaPadula, Biba, Clark-Wilson, Brewer-Nash
