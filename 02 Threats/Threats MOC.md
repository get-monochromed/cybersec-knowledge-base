# Threats & Actors

Who attacks, with what resources, and how we learn about them. Every control decision changes depending on this: an insider needs different defences than a nation-state.

| [[Hacktivist]] | low–medium | moderate | ideology |
| [[Cybercriminal]] | medium–high | high, organised, RaaS | money |
