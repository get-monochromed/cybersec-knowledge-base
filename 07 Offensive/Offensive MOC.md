# Offensive Security

Attacking systems with permission, to find what a real attacker would find before they do. Legally that permission is everything — see [[Rules of Engagement]].

## Methodology

1. [[Rules of Engagement]] and scoping — before anything technical.
2. [[Reconnaissance]] — passive, then active.
3. [[Scanning and Enumeration]] — services, versions, users.
4. [[Exploitation]] — gain a foothold.
5. [[Post Exploitation]] — privilege, persistence, movement, objectives.
6. Reporting and retesting.

## Practice modes

- [[Penetration Testing]] — time-boxed, scope-bound assurance.
- [[Red Teaming]] — objective-based adversary emulation.
- [[Purple Teaming]] — attack + detection engineering together.
- [[Bug Bounty]] — continuous external testing via disclosure programmes.
