# Certifications

What each actually proves — and what it does not. Certificates are filters, not competence.

## Entry / foundational

- **CompTIA Security+** — vocabulary and breadth. Good baseline for HR filters; no depth.
- **CompTIA Network+** — networking fundamentals; useful prerequisite.
- **ISC2 CC** — free entry cert, basic concepts.

## Offensive

- **eJPT (INE)** — practical entry-level pentest; cheap, hands-on.
- **CEH (EC-Council)** — broad, theory-heavy, expensive; recognised by HR in India and government roles.
- **OSCP (OffSec)** — the practical benchmark: 24-hour hands-on exam. Proves you can exploit real machines.
- **OSCP+ / OSEP / OSWE** — advanced exploitation, evasion, web.
- **PNPT (TCM)** — practical, report-focused, good value.
- **GPEN / GWAPT (SANS)** — expensive, deep, respected in enterprise.

## Defensive

- **CompTIA CySA+** — blue team fundamentals.
- **GCIH / GCIA / GCFA (SANS)** — incident handling, intrusion analysis, forensics; the gold standard (and priced accordingly).
- **Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1)** — practical SOC skills, good value.
- **CDSA / SOC analyst paths (HTB Academy)** — hands-on alternatives.

## Management / GRC

- **CISSP** — broad management + technical; requires 5 years experience; the classic senior credential.
- **CISM** — security management, governance.
- **CISA** — IS audit.
- **CRISC** — risk and information systems control.
- **ISO 27001 Lead Implementer / Lead Auditor** — practical ISMS credentials.

## Cloud

- **AWS Certified Security – Specialty**, **Azure AZ-500**, **Google Professional Cloud Security Engineer**, **CCSP (ISC2)**.

## Reality check

- Practical certs (OSCP, BTL1, cloud labs) predict capability better than multiple-choice ones.
- For India: CEH and CISSP carry weight in government/enterprise procurement and HR; OSCP carries weight with technical interviewers.
- Home lab + writeups + CTFs beat a certificate wall in interviews ([[Learning Resources]]).
